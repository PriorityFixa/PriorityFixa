The financial problem

Your success should not automatically increase your platform bill.

Percentage-based ticketing fees may look small on one transaction. But when you repeatedly run programmes, workshops and events, those percentages become a recurring business cost.

5% Example transaction fee used below. Your actual rate may be different.
Participants Ticket price 5% fee each Total fee
50 KES 1,000 KES 50 KES 2,500
100 KES 1,000 KES 50 KES 5,000
200 KES 1,000 KES 50 KES 10,000
KES 50,000

At a 5% fee, ten events with 100 participants paying KES 1,000 each would generate KES 50,000 in platform fees. At 10%, that same activity would cost KES 100,000. The point is not that platforms are unnecessary — it is that recurring percentage costs deserve a business-level review.
